About

A short intro from the About page on growthgame.co

I’m Trang Suwannasilp. I graduated from Chulalongkorn University in 2002 with a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering.

I spent nearly a decade as a well services engineer at PTT Exploration and Production (PTTEP).

In 2016, I began working as a senior strategic planner at PTTEP. In 2021, I became the head of the strategic planning team for AI & Robotics Ventures (ARV).

Beyond strategy work, I care deeply about gamification. I’m the author of Gamification: Motivating People with Game Mechanics (“เกมมิฟิเคชัน จูงใจคนด้วยกลไกเกม”), published by SALT Publishing.

I’m also a board game designer, and Thailand’s first Catan champion.
